## About

Done-for-you content studio for founders and coaches: video editing, LinkedIn posts, blogs, ads, websites and software. One team, one bill.

EchoPulse Media is a done-for-you content studio for founders, coaches, real-estate agents, DTC brands and local business owners. One team handles video editing, LinkedIn ghostwriting, blog production, ad creative, websites and funnels, automations and custom software, so clients stop managing five vendors. Owner-operated: the founder reviews every deliverable, replies within 3 hours on workdays, and redoes work until the client would post it under their own name. Work starts with a $299 two-week Pilot with real deliverables, then month-to-month plans from $399. Standard turnaround is 48 hours per deliverable, with unlimited revisions and 14 days notice to cancel. Founded in 2025 in Bhopal, India, serving clients in the US, UK, Canada, Australia and Western Europe.
